# Governance

pacer has one maintainer: [@lamtuanvu](https://github.com/lamtuanvu). The maintainer makes final decisions on scope, design, releases and contribution acceptance after discussion with contributors.

Propose changes through issues or pull requests; use Discussions for broader questions. Decisions should explain their reasoning, with durable architectural choices recorded in `docs/decisions/`. Participation follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
